Company overview

The Company operates three cold-storage facilities in the Midwest (Des Moines, Iowa; Joliet, Illinois; and Fort Wayne, Indiana) with 21 million cubic feet of combined capacity. Customers are regional food processors and grocery distributors on contracts averaging 3.4 years. Average occupancy over the last twelve months was 88%.

Investment highlights

  • Contracted, recurring storage revenue with the top ten customers on multi-year agreements.
  • Hard-asset coverage: owned real estate at all three sites appraised at $52 million.
  • Expansion capex is funded from proceeds, not from future cash flow.

Key considerations

  • Adjusted EBITDA of $9.5 million includes $1.4 million of add-backs over reported EBITDA of $8.1 million, mainly start-up losses at the Joliet expansion and one-time consulting fees.
  • Energy is the largest variable cost; a sustained 20% rise in power prices would reduce EBITDA by about $0.9 million.
  • The largest customer contributes 17% of revenue; its contract renews in 2028.

Arranger contact: Deal lead, Arranger capital markets desk (fictional). Commitments due within 15 business days of this memo.
